Curiosity

Timtim Jojo is a personal technology space built around curiosity, experimentation and the desire to understand how digital technology can be used to create useful things.

The idea

Learn by building.

Technology changes quickly. Instead of simply watching it happen, Timtim Jojo is about exploring it firsthand. Ideas become experiments, experiments become projects, and projects become lessons.

This website is the home for that journey — a place to document things being built, technologies being explored, and ideas that might eventually become something bigger.
