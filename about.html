Timtim Jojo is a personal technology space built around curiosity, experimentation and the desire to understand how digital technology can be used to create useful things.

The idea

Technology changes quickly. Instead of simply watching that change happen, this space is about exploring it — learning how things work, experimenting with new ideas and turning interesting concepts into real projects.

Some experiments will work. Others won't. That's part of the process. The goal is to keep learning, building and sharing what comes out of the journey.
