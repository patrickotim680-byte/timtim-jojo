01 / About the project

A place to build.

Timtim TechLab is the experimental side of Timtim Jojo. It is where technology ideas can be explored without needing to be perfect from the beginning.

The goal is simple: experiment, learn, improve and eventually turn the strongest ideas into useful projects.
